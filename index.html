Life-safety service

Fire doors are required to be tested.
We provide the proof.

Fire-rated door inspections are essential — and required — for commercial facilities. Our team tests rolling, sliding and swing-style fire doors, documents condition and functionality, and hands you a report your inspector, insurer and maintenance team can actually use.

  1. A

    Operational test

    Each door is cycled and drop-tested as conditions allow, with release devices and closing force verified.

  2. B

    Rated components

    Labels, fusible links, guides, hardware and clearances checked against the door’s listing.

  3. C

    Clear reporting

    A written summary of deficiencies, photographed and prioritised, with recommended corrective actions.

  4. D

    Follow-through

    We quote and complete the repairs we recommend — one accountable party from inspection to sign-off.

Do fire doors really need testing?

Yes. Fire-rated door assemblies in commercial facilities are required to be inspected and tested, with results documented. Rolling, sliding and swing-style fire doors each have specific checks — operational test, rated components, clearances and release devices. We provide that inspection and a written report you can keep on file.

Do you handle residential work too?

We do. Entry doors, residential overhead doors, openers, springs, cables and general repair — the same crew and the same standard we bring to commercial facilities.

What happens when a door fails after hours?

Emergency service is available. Call (603) 921-4793 and describe the failure — a door stuck open on a loading dock gets prioritised over a cosmetic issue.

Which brands do you supply and service?

We work with Hörmann, Dynaco high-speed doors, General Doors, WireCrafters partitions, Handle-It, TY Safety products, BEA sensors, Vestil equipment and LiftMaster operators, among others. If your door is a brand we don’t carry, we can usually still service it.

Is a maintenance plan worth it?

For a facility with doors in daily use, yes. Regular check-ups and tune-ups prevent unexpected failures, extend door life and keep safety devices working. We build plans around your traffic and critical openings.

How do I get a quote?

Call, email, or use the request form below. Include the door type, approximate size, location and a couple of photos if you can — that’s usually enough for us to scope the visit.
